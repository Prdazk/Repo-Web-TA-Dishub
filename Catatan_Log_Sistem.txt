Kendaraan terdeteksi tanpa track_id (fallback)
Select-String -Path logs/system.log -Pattern "2026-07-01.*FALLBACK"

Kendaraan yang di-skip karena sudah dihitung (hindari double count)
Select-String -Path logs/system.log -Pattern "2026-07-01.*SKIP"

Filter per CCTV tertentu + tanggal
Select-String -Path logs/system.log -Pattern "2026-07-01.*\[cctv_1\]"

Semua error/warning (misal stream putus)
Select-String -Path logs/system.log -Pattern "2026-07-01.*(ERROR|WARNING)"

Simpan hasil filter ke file baru (buat lampiran sidang)
Select-String -Path logs/system.log -Pattern "2026-07-01.*NEW vehicle" | Out-File hasil_new_vehicle.txt
